# WristGPT - Complete Feature Documentation

## Overview

**WristGPT: AI for Watch** is a hands-free AI assistant designed specifically for Apple Watch that brings the power of GPT right to your wrist. The app enables users to ask questions, capture notes, and journal hands-free with no phone required, providing fast, discreet access to AI designed for real life.

## Core Philosophy

WristGPT is built for professionals, runners, parents, commuters, and anyone seeking less screen time with instant, hands-free AI access. The app emphasizes speed, convenience, and privacy-first design.

## Core Features

### 1. **Hands-Free AI Assistant**
- Chat with AI using voice, swipe, or type
- Capture ideas, tasks, and journal entries instantly
- No phone required - complete standalone functionality on Apple Watch
- Real-time streaming replies for natural conversation flow

### 2. **Multiple Input Methods**
- **Voice Input** - Speak naturally to interact with AI
