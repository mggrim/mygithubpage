# WatchGPT (Petey) - Complete Feature Documentation

## Overview

WatchGPT, now rebranded as **Petey - AI Chat**, is a ChatGPT-powered application for Apple Watch, iPhone, and iPad developed by Hidde van der Ploeg. The app brings OpenAI's ChatGPT directly to your wrist and other Apple devices.

## Core Features

### 1. **ChatGPT on Apple Watch**
- Direct access to ChatGPT from your Apple Watch
- Query OpenAI's chatbot by typing or using voice-to-text input
- Quick answers without needing to pull out your iPhone
- Works on Apple Watch Series 4 and newer (requires watchOS 9+)

### 2. **Conversational AI**
- Contextual conversations - Petey remembers previous queries in the conversation
- Multi-turn dialogues with reply capability
- Multiple prompts within a single conversation thread

### 3. **Voice Capabilities**
- Voice-to-text input for hands-free queries
- Text-to-speech output - Petey reads answers aloud to you
- Quick voice commands for efficient interaction

### 4. **Watch Face Complications**
- Quick access complication for your Apple Watch face
- Customizable complication icons
- Easy one-tap access without navigating through apps

## Platform-Specific Features

### Apple Watch
- Native watchOS integration
- Optimized interface for small screen
- Standalone functionality without iPhone nearby
- Conversation history accessible on wrist

### iPhone & iPad
- **Live Activity Support** (iOS exclusive)
  - Starts when you interact with Petey
  - Continue other tasks while waiting for responses

- **Full Conversation Sharing**
  - Share entire conversations via text, email, or social media

- **Rich Previews**
  - Direct links for detected URLs
  - Rich URL previews

- **iPad-Specific Features**
  - Apple Pencil support
  - Annotate photos or draw pictures
  - Ask questions about images you've marked up
  - Vision capabilities with image analysis

## Integration Features

### 5. **Apple Music Integration**
- Create playlists with AI assistance
- Direct integration with Apple Music library
- Smart playlist generation based on natural language requests

### 6. **Apple Maps Integration**
- View locations mentioned in conversations
- Direct Apple Maps integration
- Location-based queries and answers

### 7. **Shortcuts Support**
- Apple Shortcuts integration
- Automate Petey interactions
- Replace Siri with Petey for certain tasks
- Create custom workflows

## Advanced AI Features

### 8. **Multiple AI Models**
- **GPT-3.5** - Default model (free)
- **GPT-4** - Advanced model (In-App Purchase required)
- **OpenAI o1 Models** - Latest reasoning models
  - o1-preview
  - o1-mini

### 9. **Conversation Management**
- Save conversation history
- View previous conversations
- Context retention across sessions
- Quick access to past queries

## Sharing & Export Features

### 10. **Multiple Sharing Options**
- Share via text message
- Share via email
- Share to social media
- Export conversation threads
- Copy responses to clipboard

## Customization Features

### 11. **Personalization**
- Customizable complication icons for watch face
- Choose your preferred AI model
- Configure voice settings
- Adjust interface preferences

## Language Support

### 12. **Multilingual Support**
- Supports 14 different languages
- Localized interface
- Multi-language query support

## Technical Requirements

### Minimum Requirements
- **Apple Watch**: Series 4 or newer
- **watchOS**: Version 9 or later
- **iPhone**: Compatible with latest iOS versions
- **iPad**: Compatible with latest iPadOS versions

## App Evolution

- **Original Name**: watchGPT
- **Current Name**: Petey - AI Chat
- **Reason for Rebrand**: Trademark considerations
- **Developer**: Hidde van der Ploeg (Modum B.V./User Camp Inc.)
- **Launch**: March 2023
- **Status**: Active and regularly updated

## Summary

Petey (formerly WatchGPT) is a comprehensive ChatGPT client that transforms the Apple Watch into a powerful AI assistant. With deep integration into Apple's ecosystem, voice capabilities, multiple AI models, and cross-platform functionality, it offers one of the most complete ChatGPT experiences available on Apple devices.
